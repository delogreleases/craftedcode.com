Your data, handled with care.

How we collect, use and protect personal data when you visit our website, talk to us, or use our software and services.

Last updated 14 November 2026

The short version

  • Your shop’s data (bills, customers, stock, prices) stays on your own computer. Our software works offline and does not send it to us.
  • We collect only what you share with us to talk, buy a licence or get support.
  • We never sell or rent your personal data.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

This website and our software are provided by CraftedSoft (“CraftedSoft”, “we”, “us”). We build billing, inventory and business software for businesses in India.

This policy explains how we handle personal data when you visit our website, contact us, buy or use our products, or receive our services. We follow the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiries and demos: your name, mobile number, email, business name, city and message, when you use our contact form or reach us on WhatsApp, phone, email or in person.
  • Billing: the name, business name, address and GSTIN needed for quotes and GST invoices.
  • Licences: your computer’s Machine ID (a code generated from the computer that identifies the installation; it does not reveal your files), the plan you buy and its dates.
  • Support: details, screenshots or files you send us, and, only with your permission, access to your computer or a copy of your data (for example a backup file or a remote session) so we can fix a problem.

Information collected automatically

  • Website: basic technical information such as browser, device type, pages visited, the site that referred you and an approximate location derived from your IP address, collected by our hosting provider and our analytics service, and used in aggregate.

What we do not collect

The business data you keep in our software, such as bills, customers, products, stock and prices, is stored on your own computer. Our products work offline and do not send this data to us. We only see it if you choose to share it with us for support.

3. How we use your information

  • To answer your enquiries and arrange demos.
  • To prepare quotes, issue invoices and receive payments.
  • To issue, renew and verify licences.
  • To install, update and support our software, and to deliver custom work.
  • To tell you about renewals, updates and important security or service notices.
  • To understand how our website is used, in aggregate, so we can improve it.
  • To keep the records required by tax and other laws, and to protect our legal rights.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to make automated decisions about you.

4. Why we are allowed to use it

We process personal data on these grounds:

  • Your consent, which you give when you contact us or share information with us. You can withdraw it at any time by writing to us. This does not affect what we did before, but we may then be unable to provide a service that depends on it.
  • To provide what you asked for, such as a licence, installation, update or support.
  • Legal obligations, such as keeping GST and accounting records.
  • Other legitimate uses permitted by law, such as responding to legal requests or protecting our rights.

5. Cookies and local storage

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

  • It remembers your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage, on your device only.
  • We use an analytics service to count visits and see which pages are useful, in aggregate. Depending on the service, it may set a cookie. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will keep working.

6. Who we share it with

We share personal data only when needed, and only with:

  • Service providers who help us run our business: messaging (WhatsApp), email, website hosting, analytics, payment processors and banks, and our accountants or auditors. They may use it only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities such as courts, tax authorities or law enforcement, when the law requires it.
  • A successor business, if our business is merged or transferred. We will tell you before your data falls under a different privacy policy.

We never sell or rent your personal data.

7. Where your data is stored

We keep our records on secured devices and services. Some service providers, for example WhatsApp, email and hosting, may store or process data outside India. Where this happens, it is done as permitted under Indian law.

8. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to business: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Customer, licence and invoice records: for as long as you are a customer, and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting laws require (currently up to 8 years).
  • Support files you send us, such as backups: deleted once the issue is resolved, unless you ask us to keep them.
  • Website analytics: kept in aggregate form only.

9. How we protect it

We use reasonable security practices: access is limited to the people who need it, our devices are password-protected and, where possible, encrypted, and our licence keys are digitally signed. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • delete it, unless we must keep it by law (for example invoice records);
  • withdraw your consent;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you are unable to;
  • resolve a complaint (grievance) about how we handle your data.

Write to us at hello@craftedsoft.in. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

11. Your customers’ data in our software

When you use our software to keep your own customers’ details (such as names and mobile numbers), you decide what is stored and why, and you are responsible for that data under the law. It stays on your computer. We only handle it if you ask us for support, and then only on your instructions.

We recommend that you keep regular backups, use the staff PINs, protect the computer with a Windows password and, where available, turn on disk encryption.

12. Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Other websites and services

Our website links to services such as WhatsApp and our download host. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. If we make important changes, we will tell you on our website or directly.
