FAQ

Questions about ChitTill.

Something else? Ask us directly.

Does ChitTill need the internet?

No. ChitTill runs entirely on your counter PC. Billing, printing, reports and backups all work without internet, and your data stays on your own computer.

What computer and printers do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) PC. ChitTill works with 80 mm and 58 mm thermal bill printers, A4 printers and barcode label printers, so any printer Windows can print to will do. We help you set it all up.

Is it GST compliant?

Yes. Bills show CGST + SGST or IGST with HSN codes, apparel slab rates are applied by price, B2B bills carry the buyer's GSTIN, and the GST report gives rate-wise and HSN-wise summaries with an Excel export.

What happens if my PC breaks?

ChitTill backs up automatically to a pen drive or a Google Drive folder. On a new PC we restore your latest backup and move your licence, so you can bill again the same day.

How does the free trial work?

Install it and bill for 7 days, free. When you're ready, we send a licence key for your PC. If a licence ever runs out, your data is never locked: reports, reprints and backups keep working.

Can ChitTill be customised for my shop?

Yes. That's the Tailored plan. We can change bill formats and reports, add features for your workflow, or adapt ChitTill for other kinds of retail.
